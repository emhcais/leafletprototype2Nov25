Quick Guide: European Captives Map

This map is designed like an interactive historical atlas. You do not need GIS experience.

1. Explore locations

Click a numbered marker. A red circle is a place of captivity, a blue diamond is a place of origin, and a purple marker contains both types.

2. Change what appears

Use “What would you like to see?” to show origins, captivity places, or both. Choose a century or enter a custom date range.

3. Find a person or place

Type a name, place, source, or record ID in the search box. Select a name to open a short archival-style record.

4. Browse the complete catalogue

Choose “View all captive records.” The catalogue includes records without map coordinates. You can filter for known or unknown locations.

5. Start again

Choose “Reset map” to restore all dates, both location types, and the initial Mediterranean view.

Historical caution: Markers reflect surviving records. A missing marker does not mean a missing record. Connection lines show documented associations, not necessarily the route travelled.

Download

The “Download complete dataset (CSV)” button provides the entire research dataset, including records that cannot be mapped.
